# Security Policy

At `BioEnv`, we take the security of our users and their data very seriously. We appreciate the efforts of security researchers and the community in helping us maintain a secure environment.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in `BioEnv`, please report it to us as soon as possible. We kindly request that you do **NOT** disclose the vulnerability publicly until we have had a chance to address it.

Please report vulnerabilities by sending an email to:

`abdelrahmanmostafa.eng@outlook.com`

In your report, please include:

-   A clear and concise description of the vulnerability.
-   Steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
-   The potential impact of the vulnerability.
-   Any proof-of-concept code or exploits.
-   Your contact information (optional, but helpful for follow-up).

## Our Commitment

Upon receiving a vulnerability report, we commit to:

-   Acknowledging receipt of your report within 2 business days.
-   Investigating the reported vulnerability promptly.
-   Keeping you informed of our progress in addressing the vulnerability.
-   Notifying you once the vulnerability has been resolved.
-   Publicly acknowledging your contribution (if you agree) in our [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/AbdelrahmanMostafa-Eng/bioenv/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) or a dedicated security advisory.

## Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

We ask that you adhere to the following guidelines when reporting vulnerabilities:

-   Do not access, modify, or destroy user data without explicit permission.
-   Do not interrupt or degrade our services.
-   Do not use automated scanning tools that could generate a high volume of traffic.
-   Do not attempt to phish, social engineer, or physically attack our users or infrastructure.
-   Provide sufficient information to allow us to reproduce and verify the vulnerability.

We believe in responsible disclosure and will work with you to ensure that vulnerabilities are addressed in a timely and secure manner.

Thank you for helping us keep `BioEnv` secure!
